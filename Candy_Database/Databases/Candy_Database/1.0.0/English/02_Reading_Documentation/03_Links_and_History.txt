[font_size=32][b]Links and history[/b][/font_size]

[ul]
A link to another page of the same database, version and language opens it in the page. Ctrl+click (Cmd+click on macOS) opens it beside it instead (see [url=page:Getting_Started/The_Interface]The interface[/url]).
A link to a website asks you first ([i]Open this link in your browser?[/i]), then opens it in your browser. Only [code]http[/code] and [code]https[/code] links work.
[/ul]

[b]Back[/b] and [b]Forward[/b] (the two arrows in the page's bar) walk through the pages you have read. With two pages open, each has its own history.

The history belongs to the database, version and language you have open: changing either starts a new one.
