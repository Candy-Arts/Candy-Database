[font_size=32][b]About menu[/b][/font_size]

Some pages are about the database rather than what it documents: a change log, credits, a license. Put them in an [code]_About[/code] folder and they are listed in the [b]About[/b] menu of the top bar, not in the page list. The search doesn't look in them either.

The menu only shows when the open database has at least one such page.


[font_size=22][b]Where to put them[/b][/font_size]
Like the [url=page:Writing_a_Database/Header_and_Footer]header[/url], an [code]_About[/code] folder can go in three places:

[ul]
the [b]database folder[/b], for pages shown in every version and language (credits, a license),
a [b]version folder[/b], for pages of that version only (its change log),
a [b]language folder[/b], for pages in that language.
[/ul]

The menu shows the pages of all three together. When two have the same name (without its number), the more specific one is used: a language's [code]Change_Log.txt[/code] replaces the version's, and the version's replaces the database's. That way a translation can replace a page without the others being listed twice.


[font_size=22][b]Writing them[/b][/font_size]
They are pages like any other (see [url=page:Writing_a_Database/Pages]Pages[/url]):

[ul]
Number prefixes set their order in the menu: [code]01_Change_Log.txt[/code], [code]02_Credits.txt[/code].
[code]@title:[/code] and [code]@order:[/code] work too.
They can show images and links, and the header and footer are shown around them.
[/ul]

Sub-folders of [code]_About[/code] are not read: the menu has no categories.


[font_size=22][b]Linking to them[/b][/font_size]
Their ids start with [code]_About/[/code]: link to a change log with [code][lb]url=page:_About/Change_Log]Change log[lb]/url][/code].
